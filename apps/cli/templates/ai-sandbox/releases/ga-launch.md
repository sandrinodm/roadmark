## Launch day

Tuesday 16 February 2027, 16:00 UTC. The run of show, owners and go/no-go criteria are in the [GA launch plan](../feature-sets/launch-ops/items/launch-plan.mdx).

## What GA means

- **Stable surfaces:** API v1, CLI 1.0, `@cloche/sdk` 1.0 and the `cloche` Python package 1.0, all with a written deprecation policy of 12 months.
- **Paid plans:** Free, Pro and Team, billed per vCPU-second and GiB-second. See [pricing plans](../feature-sets/billing/items/pricing-plans.mdx).
- **Support:** an API availability SLO of 99.9%, a public status page and a staffed support queue.
- **Security:** a third-party penetration test of the isolation boundary and a SOC 2 Type I report available under NDA.

## Not in GA

Team workspaces with roles, a second region and OpenTelemetry export move to [Teams and regions](teams-and-regions.md). GPU sandboxes stay exploratory.
