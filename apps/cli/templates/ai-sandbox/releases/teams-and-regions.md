## Why this is next

In beta, 38% of lost enterprise evaluations cited either missing role-based access or no EU data residency. Both are cheaper to build after GA than to delay GA for them.

## Scope

- Team workspaces with Owner, Admin, Developer and Billing roles.
- `fra` (eu-central) as a second region with region-pinned snapshots.
- Spend limits with hard stops.
- OTLP export of sandbox logs, metrics and traces.
- SOC 2 Type II observation window starts.
